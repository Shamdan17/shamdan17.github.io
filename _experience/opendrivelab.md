## Location

Shanghai, China

## Duration

July 2024 - March 2025

## About the Lab

OpenDriveLab at Shanghai AI Lab is a leading research lab focused on autonomous driving, computer vision, and large-scale AI systems. The lab develops cutting-edge approaches for self-driving vehicles and publishes at top-tier conferences.

## My Role

As a Doctoral Research Intern under the supervision of Asst. Prof. Hongyang Li, I worked on:

- **Dual-system approaches to autonomous driving** with large vision-language models
- Proposed an architecture that utilizes slow, heavy planners in tandem with lightweight planners
- Achieved **state-of-the-art performance** in driving benchmarks with **6x speedup** compared to previous approaches
- Published the work "ETA: Efficiency through Thinking Ahead" at **ICCV 2025**, available [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07725)
